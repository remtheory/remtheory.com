Judicial retention

Yes keeps the justice on the bench for a new term; no removes them. No one runs against them.

Why I'm voting yes on every judge. These are retention votes, so nobody is running against these justices and a no vote would simply remove them from the bench. I looked for organized campaigns against any of them, public discipline records, or serious controversies, and came up empty for all 17. Both Supreme Court justices have clean records, and the 15 appeals court justices have no opposition or red flags that I could find. Two of them, Lisa Rodriguez and Juliet Macaulay, were just appointed this August, so there isn't much of a track record yet. My rule of thumb is to keep a judge unless there's a real reason not to, and this year I didn't find one.
